# Sample video

`bangalore_traffic_cc0.mp4` is 8 seconds, from 1:16, of
[Bangalore 20220814 155101](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bangalore_20220814_155101.webm)
by L. Shyamal on Wikimedia Commons, released under
[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It was resized to 1280×720 for the public
demo.

The clip shows real vehicles on a public road. It is used here only to demonstrate plate
recognition.
